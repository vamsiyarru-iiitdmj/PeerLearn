## 💡 The Big Idea

Every college student knows the feeling: you're working on an assignment or preparing for exams at midnight, you hit a roadblock on an algorithm, circuit problem, or proof, and you have nowhere to turn. Professors aren't available at 11 PM, and standard discussion forums are either too slow or impersonal.

At the same time, there is a classmate just down the hall who mastered that exact topic yesterday.

**PeerLearn removes the friction from campus peer tutoring.** We believe learning isn't a one-way street where some people are permanently "teachers" and others are "students". **Every student excels at something, and everyone needs guidance at some point.** 

PeerLearn lets you ask questions without hesitation when you need help, and step into the tutor role to teach juniors or peers when you've nailed a subject.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🔄 1. Seamless Dual-Mode Experience (Student ↔ Tutor)
- **Zero rigid role lock-in:** You don't sign up as "just a student" or "just a tutor". A single click on the top-right switcher flips your dashboard from **Learner Mode** to **Mentor Mode**.
- **Student Onboarding:** Interactive walkthrough to guide you through setting up your profile, adding skills, and posting your first doubt.
- **Tutor Onboarding:** Pick your mastered skills from a curated catalog (inspired by industry taxonomies). No gatekeeping exams—your reputation is earned through real feedback from students you help.

---

### ❓ 2. Smart Doubts & Peer Matching
- **Targeted Skill Matching:** Tutors only receive doubts tagged with skills they have chosen to mentor in.
- **Tutor Availability Preview:** When students ask a doubt, they can immediately see how many active tutors are available for those tags, along with their average and peak proficiency ratings.
- **Expected Outcome Alignment:** Students define what they want to achieve before the session starts, ensuring focused, productive meetings.
- **Instant Doubt Locking:** When a tutor accepts a doubt, it automatically clears from other tutors' feeds to eliminate double-booking.
- **Pre-Session Chat & Direct Logistics:** Built-in messaging to finalize online meeting links or offline campus locations (hostel study rooms, library, etc.).
- **Accountability First:** Tutors cannot cancel booked sessions casually without providing an alternative or mutually agreeing with the learner.

---

### ⭐ 3. Crowdsourced Proficiency & Fair Ratings
- **Verified Completion:** When a session ends, the student confirms that the session actually happened and provides an honest review.
- **No Pressure, No Retaliation:** Tutor ratings are aggregated anonymously and updated on a **weekly cadence** so neither party feels uncomfortable or pressured.
- **Fair Meritocracy:** A tutor’s proficiency score rises through consistency, clarity, and positive ratings, giving top campus mentors the recognition they deserve.

---

### 📚 4. Structured Resource Buckets & Campus Commons
- **Personal Buckets ("Your Resources"):** Organize study material cleanly by topic (e.g., *Operating Systems*, *Data Science*, *DSA*). Inside each bucket, content is neatly grouped into dedicated spaces:
  - 📄 PDFs & Lab Guides
  - 🔗 Curated Articles & Video Links
  - 🖼️ Diagrams & Cheatsheets
  - 📝 Notes & Snippets
- **Democratic Campus Commons ("Shared Resources"):** Access campus-wide collections of Previous Year Questions (PYQs), exam prep kits, and open-source project guides.
- **Community Voted Submissions:** Anyone can suggest a new shared resource or bucket. The campus community gets a **24-hour voting window** to accept or decline the addition, keeping the commons high quality and spam-free.

---

### 📊 5. Verified Academic Profiles & CPI Target Planner
- **Campus Email & Academic Sync:** Sign-up is verified via OTP sent to the official institute email. Academic credentials (branch, degree, semester-wise CPI, and overall CGPA) are synced directly with the institute's academic office records.
- **CGPA Goal Setter:** Input your target graduation CGPA, and PeerLearn automatically computes the required CPI you need to maintain across your upcoming semesters.

---

### 📝 6. Smart To-Do List & "Ask Suggestion"
- Plan your semester milestones, project deadlines, and daily revision.
- Stuck on a complex syllabus goal? Tap **"Ask Suggestion"** right from your to-do card. Peer tutors will share practical workflows, study paths, and recommended resources to help you tackle the task efficiently.

---

### 📈 7. Campus Analytics & Leaderboards
- **Tag & Subject Trends:** Real-time visibility into which subjects students are finding most challenging across batches (1st year, 2nd year, etc.).
- **Tutor Hall of Fame:** Celebrates the most impactful student mentors on campus with leaderboard rankings and milestone badges.
- **Academic Health Check:** Helps student bodies and faculty identify topic bottlenecks that need extra attention.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
Make sure you have Node.js 18+ installed on your system.

### 1. Clone the repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/vamsiyarru-iiitdmj/PeerLearn.git
cd peerlearn
```

### 2. Switch to your development branch
```bash
git checkout praneeth
```

### 3. Install dependencies
```bash
npm install
```

### 4. Run the development server
```bash
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser to view the application.
